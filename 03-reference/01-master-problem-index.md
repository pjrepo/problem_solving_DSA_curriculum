# Master Problem Index

Every problem assigned or worked in this curriculum, with the week(s) it appears in.

**Generated from the week files.** If you change a week file, regenerate this rather than editing it by hand — that is what keeps the two consistent.

---

## How to read this

- **Week column** lists every week the problem appears in. A problem appearing in more than one week is almost always deliberate — either **spaced revision** (re-solve from blank), or a **re-solve with a different tool** (e.g. LC 215 by quickselect in Week 7 and by heap in Week 10).
- **Difficulty** is LeetCode's rating, not this course's. Several "Easy" problems here (LC 543 Diameter, LC 110 Balanced Binary Tree) are genuinely Medium in practice.

## ⚠ Two things to verify before handing lists to students

1. **LeetCode numbers can drift; titles do not.** The title is the reliable identifier. Spot-check numbers against leetcode.com before publishing a list.
2. **No LeetCode Premium problem is assigned anywhere in this curriculum.** This is deliberate — the free tier covers everything. Where the classic problem for a topic is Premium, a free equivalent is used instead:
